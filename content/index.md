# Aditya Soni

I'm an M.Sc. student at the [RLAI lab](http://rlai.ualberta.ca) at the University of Alberta, advised by [Prof. Martha White](https://webdocs.cs.ualberta.ca/~whitem/). My research focuses on **reinforcement learning**, specifically designing robust and reliable algorithms.

Previously, I was a predoctoral fellow at [Microsoft Research India](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/lab/microsoft-research-india/). With the M365 Research team, I applied reinforcement learning to improve systems such as Microsoft Teams and Azure datacenters.
